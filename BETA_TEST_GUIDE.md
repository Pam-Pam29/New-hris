# HRIS Beta Test Guide

Welcome to the beta cycle! This document keeps everyone aligned on what to test, how to access the environments, and what “good” looks like. Please read through each section before starting.

---

## 1. Environments
- **HR Admin Portal (Prod):** `https://hr-platform-l54uor6q2-pam-pam29s-projects.vercel.app`
- **Employee Portal (Prod):** `https://hris-employee-platform-qg05c29xe-pam-pam29s-projects.vercel.app`
- **Careers Portal (Prod):** `https://hris-careers-platform-jyjykiok4-pam-pam29s-projects.vercel.app`
- **Firestore Console:** Project `hris-system-baa22` → Collections (`companies`, `hrSettings`, `employees`, etc.) – read-only reference only; don’t edit during beta unless QA lead instructs you.

---

## 2. Create a Fresh Company (Clean Slate)
1. Open the HR portal and click **Create Company**.
2. Use a brand-new email (not used in previous rounds) and follow the signup flow.
3. During onboarding, when the form asks for **Company Domain**, enter something unique (example: `betaexample.com`).  
   - This domain is normalized into the **company slug** (e.g., `betaexample`) and stored automatically.
4. Complete every step of the onboarding wizard. Watch for:
   - Branding colours applied in real time.
   - Departments/leave types created successfully (check console logs if something feels off).
5. Once you land on the HR dashboard, note the company name and slug for later use.

> Need to confirm the slug? Go to **Settings → General → Portal Links** (see below) or, if necessary, look in Firestore under `companies/<companyId>.settings.employeeSlug`.

---

## 3. HR Admin Validation – New Company
### 3.1 Portal Links Card (Slug Confirmation)
1. In the HR portal, open **Settings → General → Portal Links**.
2. Expected results for the new company:
   - `Careers Portal` shows `https://hris-careers-platform-…/careers/<slug>` with working **Copy** and **Open** buttons.
   - `Employee Portal` shows `https://hris-employee-platform-…/employee/<slug>` plus a note like `Slug: /employee/<slug> (used for setup links)`.
   - If you see “Generated after company onboarding,” wait a minute or refresh once; otherwise, flag it.
3. Record the URLs; testers will need them for the careers/employee portal checks.

### 3.2 Employee Invitation Flow (HR Side)
1. Go to **Core HR → Employee Management** and create a new employee (minimal fields are fine).
2. After saving, use **Generate Setup Link** (same page).
3. Confirm the dialog shows `/employee/<slug>/setup?...`. Copy the URL—the employee tester will need it.
4. Optional: Check Firestore `hrSettings/<companyId>` to confirm `employeePortalUrl` and `employeeSlug` match what the UI shows.

---

## 4. Employee Portal Walkthrough (Using the New Setup Link)
### 4.1 Account Setup
1. Paste the setup link into an incognito/private window.
2. Follow the password creation screen and sign in.
3. Expected: You drop directly into the onboarding wizard (no dashboard flicker).

### 4.2 Onboarding Wizard Steps
Progress through the steps, verifying content and transitions:
1. **Welcome Video**
2. **Review Your Contract** (ensure the autogenerated contract loads; use the download button)
3. **Upload Signed Contract** (use any small file or skip according to UI prompts)
4. **System Overview**
5. **Welcome Aboard!** (completion screen summarises the four steps)

After the final screen click-through, you should land on the employee dashboard without any errors.

### 4.3 Profile Completeness Gate
1. From the dashboard, attempt to open Performance or Leave modules.
2. If your profile completeness is < 75%, you’ll be redirected back to Profile with a message—this is expected.
3. Fill out fields on the Profile page (Personal, Contact, Work, Banking) until the completeness meter updates.
4. Retry the gated modules; access should now be granted.

---

## 5. Careers Portal Verification
1. Use the `Careers Portal` link captured in §3.1 (or open it directly from the card).
2. Expected:
   - URL resolves to the new company’s slug (branding should match onboarding colours/logo if uploaded).
   - Careers SPA loads without 404s; navigation works.
   - If there are no job posts yet, the empty state renders cleanly.
3. Need to see the configured slug? It is displayed in the HR Settings card or can be found in Firestore (`hrSettings/<companyId>.careersSlug`).

---

## 6. Regression Spot-Checks (Quick sweep)
- **HR Time Management:** Employee dropdowns show names instead of “Loading…”.
- **Employee Dashboard:** Leave balances aggregate correctly (no duplicates or odd totals).
- **Goal Overdue Labels:** Goals past their due dates appear with the overdue badge.
- **Contract Editor:** Editing and saving a contract persists terms and regenerates setup link when status changes.

---

## 7. Reporting Issues
When you hit a bug, capture:
- Steps taken
- Expected vs. actual behaviour
- Screenshots or console logs (if available)
- The account/company slug you used

Send findings via the shared beta feedback channel or file them in the issue tracker tagged `beta`.

---

## 8. After Testing
- Sign out of both HR and Employee portals.
- If you created throwaway employees, leave them in place; we’ll reuse them for future rounds.
- Share a quick summary of your coverage (which sections you completed).

Thanks for helping us tighten the release—your feedback is critical! 💪
